Welcome to the About Page, Here's what you need to know about me

Name Jecil Godwin
Age 16 years old

I've been comfortable around computers for as long as I can remember. Long before I understood what code was, I was the one figuring out settings, fixing small glitches, and poking around apps just to see how they worked. That early curiosity never really went away, it just found a better direction once I discovered software development.

Learning that websites and apps are built with actual programming languages changed the way I saw every screen I touched. Instead of just using an app, I started wondering what was going on behind it, how a button click turns into an action, how a page is laid out, how data moves from one place to another. That question is what pulled me into HTML, CSS, and the wider world of programming, and it's what keeps me building. This portfolio is one of the first real steps in that journey: a space where I get to show, not just tell, what I've been learning to do.
