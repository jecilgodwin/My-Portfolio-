Achievements

Milestones Along the Way

The project I'm proudest of so far is Black Pebble Inc, the first site I built that felt like real client work rather than a practice page. Getting the header, hero section, and service cards to feel consistent and professional, rather than just functional, taught me more about structure and visual hierarchy than any tutorial had up to that point. It was the project where CSS stopped feeling like decoration and started feeling like a tool for communication.

TasteBuds Café and Wanderlust Travel Blog matter just as much, even though they're simpler, because they were the projects where the basics finally clicked: clean HTML structure, a stylesheet that stays organized instead of turning into a mess, and layouts that hold up instead of falling apart on a different screen size. Building this six-page portfolio itself, with every page linked, one shared stylesheet, and a consistent navigation and footer across all of them, is an achievement in its own right. It's proof that I can plan a small site, not just style a single page, and that's exactly the kind of milestone I want to keep building on.
